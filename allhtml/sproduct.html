product Details

The Oudh Khmer candle is a luxurious soy wax candle that combines traditional and modern fragrances for a rich sensory experience. It is Sandalwood and Agarwood in the middle, and finishes with a base of White Musk and Vanilla. Its high-quality wax ensures a clean burn, releasing layers of complex scents that enhance any space with elegance. the Parme Luxe candle helps you enhance your moment of mindfulness, yoga or meditation. The candle is made of cement, which gives it a unique and durable structure. It is designed with reusability in mind, as the candle holder can be used to plant trees and last for a long time.
